Methods and facilities

How I work

Validated simulation, data-driven modelling and laboratory measurement. I want my PhD work to combine all three.

♨

Two-phase and thermal simulation

  • ANSYS Fluent: volume of fluid interface tracking, Lee-type phase change, compiled user-defined functions for mass and energy source terms, conjugate heat transfer
  • Turbulence: SST k-omega and realizable k-epsilon with near-wall resolution checks
  • Verification: hexahedral meshing in ICEM CFD, mesh independence, Grid Convergence Index, validation against published data
  • Other solvers: COMSOL Multiphysics, Abaqus
▲

Data-driven modelling

  • Machine learning: Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, random forest, Gaussian process and four other regressors with cross-validation
  • Explainability: Shapley values, partial dependence, individual conditional expectation
  • Optimization: Latin hypercube sampling, response surfaces, Pareto knee points, ANSYS optiSLang
  • Correlations: dimensionless Nusselt number correlations from simulation data
★

Laboratory experience

  • Heat exchangers: TecQuipment TD1007 and Edibon concentric-tube rigs: log-mean temperature difference, heat transfer coefficient, effectiveness
  • Phase change: TecQuipment TE78 and Plint boiling apparatus, filmwise and dropwise condensation
  • Refrigeration: Plint vapour-compression and heat-pump rigs
  • Measurement: TD1003 radiant heat transfer, TD200 engine testing, heat flux measurement of insulation panels
⚙

Design and programming

  • CAD: SolidWorks (Certified SOLIDWORKS Professional), AutoCAD
  • Programming: Python (NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn), C
  • Structures: fused filament 3D printing, quasi-static compression and Abaqus nonlinear finite element models
  • Materials testing: tensile and flexural tests, SEM and EDS interpretation, water absorption
