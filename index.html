How I work
From physics to design, in five steps

Every study follows the same loop: build a model I can trust, learn what the physics is doing, then turn it into a faster design tool.

🎯

Define

Pick the thermal question and the operating window that matters.

💻

Simulate

Two-phase and conjugate heat transfer models with custom source terms.

✅

Validate

Mesh studies, uncertainty and comparison with experimental and literature data.

🧠

Learn

Surrogates and explainable machine learning on the simulation data.

🚀

Design

Correlations, Pareto-optimal designs and clear design rules.
